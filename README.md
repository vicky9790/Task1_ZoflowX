## Opportunity Radar – AI-Powered Business Signal Intelligence

### Overview

Opportunity Radar is an AI-powered business intelligence platform that identifies and prioritizes companies based on business growth signals such as funding announcements, hiring activity, market expansion, product launches, partnerships, and technology adoption.

The platform helps business development and sales teams focus on high-potential opportunities by automatically analyzing company signals, calculating opportunity scores, and generating AI-powered recommendations.

---

## Problem Statement

Companies generate various public signals that indicate growth and expansion.

Examples include:

* Funding Announcements
* Hiring Activity
* Product Launches
* Market Expansion
* Partnerships
* Technology Adoption

The challenge is identifying which companies are most likely to become potential customers and prioritizing outreach efforts.

---

## Solution

Opportunity Radar analyzes company signals and:

* Calculates Opportunity Scores
* Prioritizes potential customers
* Generates AI-powered business analysis
* Suggests outreach strategies
* Creates prospecting email drafts

---

## Features

### Business Signal Detection

Detects and analyses:

* Funding Rounds
* Hiring Activity
* Product Launches
* Market Expansion
* Technology Adoption
* Strategic Partnerships

### Opportunity Scoring Engine

Weighted scoring model:

| Signal | Impact |
| ------------------- | ------ |
| Funding Round | +30 |
| Talent Scaling | +20 |
| Market Expansion | +15 |
| Product Launch | +25 |
| Technology Adoption | +10 |

Scores are capped between 0 and 100.

### AI-Powered Analysis

Using Google Gemini API:

* Business Opportunity Assessment
* Opportunity Justification
* Recommended Actions
* Service Recommendations
* Prospecting Email Generation

### Outreach Campaign Copilot

Provides:

* Recommended Actions
* Target Offering Suggestions
* Automated Prospecting Emails

---

## Tech Stack

Frontend:

* React
* Vite
* JavaScript
* CSS

AI:

* Google Gemini API

Development Tools:

* VS Code
* Git
* GitHub

---

## Workflow

Business Signals → Opportunity Scoring Engine → Gemini AI Analysis → Opportunity Prioritization → Recommended Actions → Prospecting Email Generation
